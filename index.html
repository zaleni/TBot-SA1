MagicBot-R0 is a World-Spatial-Action model for robot control. It learns temporal 2D-3D scene evolution and maps the learned spatial world representation to executable action chunks.

Abstract

Bridging 2D-centric visual learning and 3D-centric embodied interaction

Embodied foundation models have evolved into Vision-Language-Action (VLA) and World-Action-Model (WAM) paradigms. Although impressive, both paradigms are inherently 2D-centric and lack innate understanding of 3D world dynamics, creating an understanding-to-execution gap for physical robot interaction. We present MagicBot-R0, a robot foundation model built upon World-Spatial-Action (WSA) modeling. WSA jointly learns latent causal reasoning from 2D observations to 3D goal-directed actions through subgoal image generation, subgoal 3D scene prediction, and 3D goal-conditioned robot action forecasting.

Method

World-Spatial-Action modeling

A robot policy should not only predict what action to take, but also learn what spatial change that action is expected to cause. MagicBot-R0 models future 2D appearance, latent future-3D scene evolution, and executable robot actions in one coupled world-action graph.

VLA policy

At ~ pi(At | ot)

Direct action prediction from observations, language, and proprioception.

World Action Model

(v+t, At) ~ p(v+t, At | ot)

Couples action learning with future visual dynamics, but remains mainly 2D.

World-Spatial-Action

(v+t, gt, At) ~ p(v+t, gt, At | ot)

Introduces latent future-3D tokens to model spatial action consequences.

Video expert

Predicts future visual latents and provides dense temporal supervision for task-relevant scene evolution.

Future-3D expert

Uses view-organized slot tokens aligned with geometry-aware latent features to model post-interaction spatial structure.

Action expert

Predicts continuous action chunks conditioned on latent future-3D tokens, yielding a 2D-3D grounded inverse dynamics formulation.

Training And Inference

Learning spatial consequences without explicit 3D supervision

Future-video flow matching

Current visual tokens condition a future-video flow objective, encouraging the model to encode how the scene should evolve under the intended task.

Future-3D latent alignment

Compact future-3D slot tokens are projected into a geometry-aware latent space and aligned to frozen target features, without depth maps or calibrated reconstruction.

Action flow matching

Continuous action chunks are optimized with a flow-matching objective and conditioned on the learned latent future-3D representation.

Closed-loop deployment

At test time, future RGB generation is removed. MagicBot-R0 denoises action chunks while recomputing latent future-3D tokens internally.
